1. The Opportunity

Every year, Iranian founders and technology teams participate in exhibitions, technology

events and startup programs. They meet Turkish companies, investors, accelerators,

technology parks and potential customers.

But after the event, a critical question remains: **"How do we legally and practically

continue in Türkiye?"** That is the gap we want to solve.

2. The Proposed Platform

We are developing a Türkiye Startup Landing Platform that moves selected international startup teams from

Exhibition → Market Entry → Turkish Business Operation → Corporate Partnership → Investment.

The first target market is: Iranian technology startups entering Türkiye.

3. Why a Legal Partner Is Essential

A startup cannot safely enter a new market with only travel support, hotel booking,

networking and AI tools. It needs a qualified legal pathway.

The platform therefore seeks a Strategic Legal Landing Partner with expertise in:

  • Iran–Türkiye cross-border matters
  • Corporate law
  • Company formation
  • Commercial contracts
  • Immigration / residence pathways
  • Employment
  • Intellectual property
  • Tax coordination
  • Investment agreements

Professional legal activity in Türkiye is regulated by the bar associations and foreign

lawyers cannot simply practise as Turkish lawyers. The partnership is therefore structured

with lawyers qualified in Türkiye, and the legal partner — not the platform — defines

and confirms every permissible pathway.

4. Proposed Role of Iranian–Turkish Legal Group

We propose establishing a dedicated Iran–Türkiye Startup Legal Desk within the broader

Startup Landing Platform. The Desk becomes the first legal point of contact for selected

startup teams entering Türkiye.

5. Startup Journey

STEP 1 — Selection

Elcom, GITEX, startup competitions, universities, accelerators, startup communities.

STEP 2 — Pre-Landing Legal Assessment

The legal partner reviews founder structure, current company, IP ownership, contracts, team members, intended activities in Türkiye and possible legal structures.

STEP 3 — Türkiye Entry

The team receives professional guidance concerning applicable visa, residence, work-authorization and company-formation pathways. No guaranteed immigration result is promised.

STEP 4 — Turkish Business Structure

Turkish subsidiary / Turkish company / branch or representative structure where appropriate / other legally suitable structure.

STEP 5 — Commercial Launch

Corporate contracts, partnership agreements, employment arrangements, IP protection, commercial compliance.

STEP 6 — Investment

Investment, shareholding, founder arrangements, due diligence, commercial partnerships.

6. Two-Layer Legal Model

Layer 1 — Market Entry Legal (before the startup arrives)

Immigration · Company · IP · Contracts · Compliance · Founder structure

Layer 2 — Investment & Growth Legal (after landing)

Corporate agreements · PoC contracts · Investment documentation · Shareholder agreements · Due diligence · Strategic partnerships

The same startup can therefore remain inside the legal partner's ecosystem as it grows.

7. Why This Is Valuable for the Legal Group

This is not simply a referral arrangement. The platform creates a

be ready to operate in Türkiye, the legal partner receives pre-screened teams with:

  • Startup profile
  • Founder information
  • Business model
  • Technology
  • Funding stage
  • Intended Turkish activity
  • Legal requirements
  • Corporate targets

The lawyer enters the process earlier.

10. Corporate Sponsor Model

A Turkish company sponsors a selected startup — e.g. a Turkish industrial company

sponsoring an Iranian industrial-AI startup. The startup enters Türkiye, the legal

partner establishes the contractual framework, and the two run a Proof of Concept.

If successful, a commercial contract, investment or strategic partnership can

follow. This turns legal work from a one-time company registration into a long-term

commercial relationship.

11. AI Layer

The platform includes an AI Startup Navigator that organises the legal checklist, market

research, corporate matching, investor research, meeting preparation, media/PR and startup

documentation.

AI does not replace the lawyer. It prepares, organises and routes information.

The licensed legal professional remains responsible for legal advice.

12. Legal AI Safety Principle

The platform explicitly distinguishes AI Information from Professional Legal Advice:

  • The AI may say: "These are the documents/questions that may need to be reviewed."
  • The legal partner decides: "This is the legally appropriate route for this specific client."

This protects the professional role of the legal partner and is enforced in code

(guarantee language is stripped; immigration determinations are escalated to a human).
